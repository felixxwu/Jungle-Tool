# Jungle Tool

A web-based drum loop slicing and sequencing tool for creating Jungle, Breakbeat, and Old-school Hip Hop.

## About

Jungle Tool is an in-browser music production tool for slicing classic drum breaks, sequencing them on a grid, layering multiple breaks, and exporting your creations. It comes with 30+ legendary break samples (Amen Brother, Think, Funky Drummer, and more) ready to use - no importing required to get started.

**Advanced users** can import their own WAV files to use alongside the built-in breaks.

## Features

### 🎵 Break Library

- **30+ Classic Breaks**: Legendary samples ready to slice
- **Import Your Own**: Add WAV files to your library (optional)
- **Auto-Slicing**: Automatically detect and slice breaks into individual hits (Kick, Snare, Hat)
- **Manual Editing**: Fine-tune slice positions, types, and step assignments

### 🎛️ Arrangement & Sequencing

- **16x16 Grid Sequencer**: Visual grid for programming beats across multiple bars
- **Real-time Playback**: Loop arrangements with precise timing
- **Waveform Visualization**: See your arrangement as a waveform

### 🎚️ Controls

- **BPM** (80-180), **Swing** (0-33%), **Saturation**, **Note Length & Fade Out**
- **Per-Layer**: Independent pitch (±12 semitones) and volume (0-100) for each break
- **Randomize Layers**: Quickly try different break combinations

## How to Use

### 1. Choose a Break

Select a break from the **Library** tab.

### 2. Add to Arrangement

Click **Add to arrangement +** to add the break to your layers. You can add multiple breaks to layer them together.

### 3. Sequence Your Beat

Go to the **Arrangement** tab and click the grid to place notes. Click again to remove them. Press **Spacebar** to play/pause.

### 4. Adjust Settings

Use the sliders in the sidebar to adjust BPM, swing, and other settings. Adjust volume and pitch for each layer individually.

### 5. Export

Click **Download** to export your arrangement as a WAV file.
